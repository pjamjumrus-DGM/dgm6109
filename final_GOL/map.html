Tsleil-Waututh Nation

People of the Inlet — Burrard Inlet to Fraser Valley

First Nations

The Tsleil-Waututh (meaning "People of the Inlet") have been stewards of Burrard Inlet since time immemorial. Their territory spans from the inlet northward through the mountains and south toward the Fraser Valley.

Musqueam Nation

xʷməθkʷəy̓əm — Fraser River Delta & South Vancouver

First Nations

The Musqueam have lived at the mouth of the Fraser River for over 4,000 years. Much of what is now Vancouver, Richmond, and UBC campus sits on Musqueam territory.
