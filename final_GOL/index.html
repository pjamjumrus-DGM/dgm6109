The Problem

What no one tells you before you arrive

International students are recruited through narratives of opportunity and multiculturalism — a framework that often obscures Canada's colonial history and the realities of settler identity.

🗺️

Unknown Colonial History

Students arrive with no awareness of the land they're settling on or Indigenous peoples' ongoing relationship to it.

📣

Misleading Recruitment

Agencies provide positive narratives devoid of historical context, housing realities, or settler responsibilities.

💔

Identity Shock After Arrival

Students discover too late that they are settlers — experiencing distress, isolation, and the sensation of having been deceived.
