Orientation Modules

Complete each module at your own pace before arriving in Canada. Each section is designed to be honest, respectful, and genuinely useful — not just a checkbox.

✓
Module 1 · Completed
The Land You're Coming To
Understanding Indigenous territories, history, and the significance of the land
⏱ 45 min

Canada is home to over 600 distinct First Nations, Métis, and Inuit communities, each with their own languages, traditions, and deep connections to specific territories.

  • ▶ Introduction to Indigenous Peoples of Canada (12 min)
  • ▶ What is a Land Acknowledgement? (8 min)
  • 📄 Reading: The History of Treaties (15 min)
  • 🗺️ Interactive: Your City's Territory Map (10 min)
◈
Module 2 · In Progress
Understanding Your Role as a Settler
What it means to arrive as a newcomer on Indigenous territory
⏱ 55 min

This module addresses something many students discover only after arrival: that coming to Canada means arriving as a settler on land with a complex, ongoing colonial history.

"What happens when you find yourself alone, navigating a new settler identity in a new country with many other challenges?"
— Grounded on Land Research, 2026
  • ▶ What is Settler Colonialism? (14 min)
  • ▶ Your Responsibilities as a Newcomer (10 min)
  • 💬 Recorded Story: International Student Experience
  • ✏️ Reflection Exercise: Your Expectations vs Reality
